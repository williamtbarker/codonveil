# Changelog

All notable changes are documented here.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-10

### Added

- Exact expansion of all DNA-IUPAC codons under the standard genetic code.
- Reference-relative classifications for ambiguity, substitution, stop risk, stop loss, and gaps.
- Strict aligned-CDS FASTA parser with DNA/RNA normalization.
- Deterministic Markdown, JSON, CSV, and TSV reports.
- Optional CI gates and documented exit codes.
- Exhaustive 3,375-codon validation and end-to-end tests.
